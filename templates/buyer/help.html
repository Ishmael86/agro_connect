Frequently Asked Questions

Browse common inquiries below or start a live request to our support desk.

Browse the products in our marketplace. Click "Add to Cart" on any crop listing. Once you have selected all desired items, click the shopping bag cart icon at the top right, verify the subtotals, and proceed to the Checkout page to enter your delivery address and choose a payment method.

Navigate to "My Orders" in the sidebar. Locate your order card and click "View Details". You will see a real-time visual progress timeline displaying stages: Order Placed, Processing, Shipped, and Delivered.

You can send inquiries directly from a farmer's public profile page. Type your inquiry into the contact card sidebar and click Send. This starts a direct message thread accessible via the "Messages" tab in your buyer dashboard.

AgroConnect matches you with local delivery drivers or farmers who coordinate drop-offs directly to your shipping address. We offer a flat shipping rate of GHS 10.00, which is completely waived (FREE) for order subtotals over GHS 300.00.

We support major Ghanaian mobile money services (MTN Mobile Money, Telecel Cash, AT Money), credit/debit card entries (Visa, Mastercard), and secure Cash on Delivery parameters.
