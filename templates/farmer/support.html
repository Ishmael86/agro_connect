Available funds can be requested directly from the Payouts page. We support withdrawals to MTN Mobile Money, Telecel Cash, AirtelTigo Money, or bank accounts. Requests are verified and processed within 24-48 business hours.

To ensure buyer security, sales revenue is held in escrow until the order status is marked as DELIVERED. Once confirmed, the items subtotal is immediately transferred to your available balance.

Go to Orders, select the specific order, and click the status action buttons at the bottom. You can advance orders from Accepted to Processing, Shipped, and finally confirm Delivery.
