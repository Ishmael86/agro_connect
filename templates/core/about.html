Growing Agriculture
Through Connection

AgroConnect is a technology-driven agricultural marketplace built to eliminate inefficiencies in the agricultural supply chain in Ghana.

Our platform enables farmers to list their fresh produce directly, negotiation-free, ensuring they receive fair, transparent prices for their hard work. At the same time, retail buyers, restaurant owners, and households can purchase high-quality produce with delivery assurance, supporting local growers directly.

Our Story

Founded in 2026, AgroConnect began as a university project addressing the severe post-harvest losses and pricing manipulation suffered by vegetable farmers in rural Ghana. We set out to build a platform that gives farmers direct market access, transforming farming into a reliable business.

Our Mission

To empower local Ghanaian farmers by connecting them directly with buyers, securing fair pricing, reducing post-harvest waste, and promoting sustainable, community-backed agriculture.

Our Vision

To become the leading agricultural commerce platform in West Africa, driving financial inclusion for smallholder farmers and building reliable food supply chains.

Our Core Values

Trust & Transparency

We establish trust through verified farmer profiles, clear product grading, and transparent pricing without hidden markups.

Freshness & Quality

We verify that all produce cataloged is harvested fresh and shipped under conditions that maintain peak quality.

Community Impact

Every purchase supports local Ghanaian farming families, promoting community resilience and youth engagement in agriculture.
