How AgroConnect Works

We provide an intuitive, direct bridge for agricultural trade. Learn how you can get started as a Buyer or a Farmer.

For Buyers

Fresh agricultural produce sourced directly from Ghanaian farms, delivered directly to your kitchen.

01

Create an Account

Register as a Buyer in seconds. Provide your delivery preferences and contact information.

02

Browse Fresh Produce

Explore products sorted by category, region, and price. View detailed farm pages and verified reviews.

03

Add to Cart & Checkout

Compile products from multiple local farms. Choose secure Mobile Money, Card, or Cash on Delivery.

04

Receive & Confirm Delivery

Our delivery partners bring the fresh produce to your door. You check the items and complete the transaction.

Start Shopping Now

For Farmers

Gain direct access to buyers in major cities. Get fair prices and scale up your farming operations.

01

Register Your Profile

Sign up as a Farmer. List your farm name, location, contact number, and region of operations.

02

Verify Your Farm

Our representatives will contact you to verify your operations and award the "Verified Farmer" badge.

03

List Your Harvest

List your crops, specify prices per unit (e.g. GHS per bag or bunch), describe stock quantity, and add images.

04

Get Paid Directly

Receive orders from retail buyers. We pick up the produce and transfer payments directly to your Mobile Money account.
